Quick answers

How do I reserve a book?

Call the front desk or use the contact form with the title and author; we will hold it for three days.

Can I renew by phone?

Yes. Have your library card number ready and renew before the due date. Items with reservations cannot be renewed.

Do you offer quiet study space?

Yes. Two study rooms can be booked for two-hour slots at the front desk or by phone.
