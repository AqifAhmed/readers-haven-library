What we offer

Space to read, learn and create.

From quiet study corners to friendly digital help, our services are free or low-cost for members. Times below are staffed hours; the building closes at the times shown on the home page.

Library services

📖

Borrowing & reservations

Monday to Saturday · 9:00 – 18:00

Borrow up to your plan limit, renew twice, or reserve a title that is currently on loan.

  1. Search the catalogue or ask a librarian.
  2. Reserve online or at the front desk.
  3. Collect within 3 days of the email.
📚

Study rooms

Daily · 9:00 – 18:30

Two quiet rooms for individuals or small groups, with power sockets and a whiteboard.

  1. Book a 2-hour slot at the front desk.
  2. Bring your library card.
  3. Cancel early so someone else can use the room.
💻

Digital help desk

Tuesday & Thursday · 15:00 – 18:00

One-to-one help with email, online forms, e-books and staying safe online.

  1. Book a free 30-minute session.
  2. Bring your device and any passwords you need.
  3. Staff will not ask for your bank details.
🎤

Children’s story time

Saturday · 10:30 – 11:15

Songs, stories and a simple craft for children aged 3 to 8 and their grown-ups.

  1. Drop in; no booking needed.
  2. Arrive five minutes early for a seat.
  3. Ask about our quiet-hour sessions.
🖨

Print, scan & copy

Monday to Saturday · all opening hours

Self-service printing and scanning with a staffed counter for larger jobs.

  1. Send files to the print queue at the desk.
  2. Pay per page: PKR 10 black & white, PKR 30 colour.
  3. Collect your pages and clear the queue.
🚚

Home delivery

Fortnightly · Friday mornings

For members who cannot visit the library because of age, illness or disability.

  1. Ask a relative or carer to call us.
  2. Tell us the genres you enjoy.
  3. We deliver a selected bag every two weeks.

Access and support

The building has step-free access, an accessible toilet and a hearing loop at the front desk. Large-print and audiobook collections are available. If you need another adjustment, tell us when you book and we will do our best.
