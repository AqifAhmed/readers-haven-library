Welcome to Readers’ Haven

A place where every reader belongs.

Borrow a book, discover a new idea, or simply find a quiet corner to read. We are a free public library serving readers of all ages in the heart of the community.

Explore the collections Become a member

Our mission

Readers’ Haven exists to make reading, learning and discovery accessible to everyone. We connect people with books, trusted information and free programmes that spark curiosity and bring neighbours together.

  • Offer an inclusive, welcoming space for all ages.
  • Build a collection that reflects our community.
  • Support literacy, learning and digital access.
