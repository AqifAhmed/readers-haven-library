Borrowing rules

  • Items can be renewed twice if nobody else has reserved them.
  • Late fees are capped at the replacement cost of the item.
  • Lost or damaged items must be replaced or paid for at the current price.
  • Reference books and the local-history archive are in-library use only.
  • Members under 16 need a parent or guardian to countersign the application.

How to join

  1. Bring your ID A CNIC, student card or passport, plus proof of address.
  2. Fill in the form Staff will help you complete the one-page application at the desk.
  3. Pay the annual fee Cash or card at the front desk; you will receive your card the same day.
  4. Start borrowing Browse the shelves or reserve a title through the contact page.
